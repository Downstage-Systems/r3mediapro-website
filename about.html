About R3 Media Production

A full-service production team trusted by event planners and marketing teams to make their events look incredible - in person and online.

Our Story

Built on production experience, not just gear

R3 Media Production started with a simple idea: event video should feel effortless for the people running the event. Planners have enough on their plate - the video team should show up prepared, stay out of the way, and deliver work that makes everyone look good.

Today we produce corporate event coverage, live streams, and commercial video for clients who care about quality and reliability. Every project gets the same treatment: careful planning, redundant systems, and editing that respects your brand.

Meet the Owner

Rob Spradling

Owner / Operator

Rob has spent 15 years in video production, including the last 6 producing live events - keynotes, conferences, and broadcasts where there are no second takes. That's the experience behind every R3 project: calm under pressure, obsessive about the details, and focused on making the people on stage look their best.

Based in Littleton, Colorado, Rob works with clients across the Denver metro and Front Range - and travels wherever the event is. When you hire R3, Rob is your point of contact from the first call to final delivery.
